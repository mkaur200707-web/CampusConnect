ABOUT CAMPUSCONNECT

Small Reports.
Big Improvements.

CampusConnect gives students a simple way to report everyday campus problems and follow what happens after they submit a report.

From classroom problems to campus facilities, students can raise an issue, track its status, and stay informed until it is resolved.

HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps.

01

Report

Found something that needs attention? Submit a campus issue with a title, category and description.

02

Track

Check your submitted issues and see whether they are Open or In Progress.

03

Resolve

Follow the status until the issue is resolved and your campus problem gets addressed.
